About

The radio environment does not have to be fixed. We can make space itself part of the design.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I received my B.Eng. and Ph.D. degrees from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2018 and 2024.

My research interests include reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, movable antennas, satellite communications, and space–air–ground integrated networks. I also serve on the IEEE Vehicular Technology Society Committee on Drones.
