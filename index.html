Research in Practice

From geometry to working systems.

Across my work, physical configuration and digital signal processing are designed together, then tested against implementation and deployment constraints.

Move the layers, not every element.

A two-layer transmissive movable intelligent surface synthesizes beam patterns through relative displacement, connecting electromagnetic architecture, optimization, and a 12.2 GHz proof of concept.

About

The radio environment does not have to be fixed. We can make space itself part of the design.

I am a postdoctoral researcher with the Department of Electronic Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I received my B.Eng. and Ph.D. degrees from the School of Information and Communication Engineering, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, in 2018 and 2024, respectively.

My research focuses on reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, movable and rotatable antennas, satellite communications, and space–air–ground integrated networks. I have served as a member of the IEEE Vehicular Technology Society Committee on Drones since 2026.
